# WEF Global Opportunity Analyzer 🌍⚡

> Python analyzer for high-value opportunities in the World Economic Forum ecosystem

Maps the massive opportunity landscape created by WEF's global initiatives — from climate finance and digital transformation to Fourth Industrial Revolution technologies and Davos-adjacent consulting plays.

## What It Does
- Models ROI for WEF initiative-aligned businesses (SDGs, Great Reset, 4IR)
- Analyzes climate finance and ESG investment opportunity sizing
- Maps digital transformation consulting opportunities for governments and corporations
- Identifies partnership and grant opportunities through WEF's Centre network
